Questions, answered.

Do you set up and run the booth?

Yes. We handle set up and tear down, and an onsite attendant stays with the booth so you can enjoy your event.

What print sizes can we choose?

Unlimited prints in either 2x6 strips or 4x6 photos.

How do we get the photos afterwards?

Guests can share digitally on the spot, there is a live gallery during the event, and we transfer all the photos to the host online.

Can we customise the booth for our event?

Yes. Every booking includes a backdrop, themed props and a custom event screen display, and add-ons are available. Message us with your idea.
